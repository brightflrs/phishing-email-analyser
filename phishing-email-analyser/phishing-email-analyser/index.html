How the score works

The score starts at 0. Each warning sign found adds a fixed number of points. Reassuring signs subtract points. The total is capped between 0 and 100.

Important safety information

  • This tool cannot determine whether an email is malicious. It only highlights warning signs. A low score does not mean an email is safe, and a high score does not prove it is an attack.
  • It works from text patterns and cannot open links, scan attachments, or check websites. It can miss sophisticated attacks and can flag genuine emails.
  • Do not click suspicious links or open attachments just to test them.
  • Verify important messages independently, using the organisation's official website, app, or a phone number you already know.
